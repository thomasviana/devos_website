# Validation strategy

Goal: Test whether online-acquired strangers sign up, engage repeatedly and pay.

Phase 1: landing page and Day 1 sample; $75–$100 message test. Phase 2: produce/review seven-day content, then launch free challenge. Phase 3: measure first session, repeat use, three sessions within seven days, and actual paid commitments.

Working total ad budget: $400, staged, not guaranteed. Separate traffic by language, channel and campaign. Focus on cost per activated and paying family, not only waitlist volume. Collect minimal data and avoid collecting children's identities for marketing.
