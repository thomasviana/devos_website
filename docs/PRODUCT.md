# Product brief

Audience: Christian parents with children ages 3–10. English and Spanish. Working name: Devos. Promise: Little moments. Lifelong faith.

MVP: audio-first school-run Bible devotionals with verse, context, questions and prayer; parent-read bedtime stories with lesson, verse and prayer. Screen-light, parent-led, Christ-centered and broadly welcoming, without denomination-specific prayers.

Future: gentle relationship reminders, family habits, children's financial literacy, thoughtful gamification and a Flutter app after validation.
