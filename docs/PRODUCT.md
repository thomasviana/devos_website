# Devos product brief

**Working name:** Devos — *Little moments. Lifelong faith.* Name/trademark availability not yet cleared.

**For:** Christian parents of children roughly ages 3–10, bilingual English/Spanish.

## Core promise
The moments are already there. Make them matter. Devos helps parents turn the school run and bedtime into meaningful moments of faith and parent-child connection.

## Experience A — Morning
- **Moment:** commuting to school, breakfast, or another ordinary morning routine.
- **Job to be done:** help a parent and child connect with Scripture and each other in ~3–5 minutes, without requiring the driver to read.
- **Interaction:** begin audio before travel; verse with context, a relatable takeaway, a few parent-child discussion prompts, and short prayer.
- **Safety:** no visual interaction while driving. Current web sample uses browser speech synthesis, which may not persist with phone locked or when backgrounded. It is a demonstration, not a driving-certified player.

## Experience B — Bedtime (equally important)
- **Moment:** child asks for a bedtime story; mom or dad reads aloud.
- **Job to be done:** connect emotionally, encourage imagination, and help a child process familiar age-related struggles through faith.
- **Interaction:** quiet, paginated low-light reading, age-relevant story, organic biblical takeaway, optional reflection and prayer.
- **Strict UX:** no animated illustrations, autoplay, progress confetti, bright colors, quiz screens, streaks, attention traps, or child-facing gamification. Parent's voice is the experience.
- Stories should have real narrative development. Children should recognize their emotions and circumstances in the character; don't reduce stories to a moral lecture.

## Theological and editorial boundaries
Bible-centered and Christ-centered, welcoming to Christian families across traditions while avoiding denomination-specific prayers (e.g., to Mary or saints). Review all Bible references, quote permissions, cultural appropriateness, age fit, and accuracy before publishing.

## Validation
The website offers two interactive Day 1 samples and a waitlist for a planned **free seven-day family challenge**. The seven-day experience and automated delivery do **not** exist yet. Do not imply otherwise.

## Future (not part of validation MVP)
Full content engine, authentication, personal preferences, reliable hosted audio, Flutter app, family routines, gentle retention, spouse/relative kindness prompts, child financial literacy.
